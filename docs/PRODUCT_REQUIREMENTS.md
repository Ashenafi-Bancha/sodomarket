# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Name
**Wolaita Marketplace** (Configurable brand: Sodo Market Hub)

## Geographic Focus
- Initial Town: **Wolaita Sodo, Ethiopia**
- Key Urban Zones: Mehal Ketema (Downtown/Civic), Merkato (Commercial Hub), Arada (Heritage & Retail), Boditi Road (Asphalt Corridor), Ligaba (Administrative/Residential), Humbo Gate (South Terminal).
- Expansion Capability: Regional zones across South Ethiopia.

## Core Asset Categories
1. **Real Estate**
   - Houses for sale & rent (compound size, bedrooms, bathrooms, water reserve, electricity)
   - Residential land & commercial plots (titled land book, m²)
   - Commercial storefronts, offices, and warehouses
2. **Vehicles**
   - Passenger cars (Toyota, Hyundai, Lifan)
   - Commercial trucks & haulers (Isuzu NPR, Toyota Hilux pickups)
   - Motorcycles (150cc transport motors)

## User Roles & Workspaces
- **Public Visitor / Buyer**: Free browsing, category search, zone filtering, WhatsApp/direct call contact, inquiry form submission, viewing scheduler.
- **Private Seller**: Multi-step wizard to post assets, declaration of ownership.
- **Licensed Broker**: CRM workspace, lead pipeline tracking (`NEW_INQUIRY`, `CONTACTED`, `VIEWING_SCHEDULED`, `NEGOTIATION`, `WON`, `LOST`), viewing schedule in Ethiopia local time, manual deal & commission tracking.
- **Platform Administrator**: Moderation review queue, approval/rejection with feedback, duplicate asset warnings, broker license verification, immutable audit log.

## Localization
- Supported: **Wolaytta** (`wal`), **Amharic** (`am`), **English** (`en`).
- Persistent locale selector in navbar.
